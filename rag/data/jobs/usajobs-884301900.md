Summary
This position is located in the Department of Health & Human Services (HHS), Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), Center for Clinical Standards and Quality,CCSQ, Survey and Operations Group (SOG). As a Senior IT Specialist (Robotic Process Automation and AI), GS-2210-15, you will report directly to the SOG Leadership and provide highly complex analysis, project management, and program direction for all Divisions within the Group. Learn more about this agency

Duties
Lead the development, implementation, and maintenance of the Survey and Operations Group's automation strategy, ensuring seamless integration with national reporting systems. Design and manage large-scale automation studies, applying advanced data analysis techniques to interpret and evaluate complex Medicare and Medicaid datasets. Develop policy recommendations and guidelines, analyze legislative proposals, and draft formal responses to reports impacting CMS survey and enforcement processes. Coordinate and represent the Group in activities with CMS components, Congress, and health organizations, negotiating among diverse stakeholders to achieve consensus. Manage large-scale projects by organizing cross-functional collaboration across CMS, government units, and advisory committees, ensuring alignment with agency policy and program goals

Conditions of employment
You must be a U.S. Citizen or National to apply for this position. You will be subject to a background and suitability investigation. Selective Service: Males born after December 31, 1959, must be registered or exempt from Selective Service-http//www.sss.gov One-year probationary period may be required. If you are selected for this position, the documentation that you present for purposes of completing the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) form I-9 will be verified through the DHS "E-Verify" System. All Federal employees are required to have Federal salary payments made by direct deposit to a financial institution of their choice.
