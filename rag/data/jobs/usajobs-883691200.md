Summary
As an Al Software Engineer you will establish technical strategies, standards, and architectures to drive secure, ethical, and engineering-sound AI adoption across the federal government. This position is with the Office of Technology Transformation Services (TTS), Federal Acquisition Service (FAS), General Services Administration (GSA). We are currently filling multiple vacancies, but additional vacancies may be filled as needed. Learn more about this agency

Duties
Serves as a principal technical expert formulating and directing high level, enterprise-wide engineering strategies for Al-enabled systems, computer hardware/software integration, and cloud-native architectures and platforms, delivering impactful solutions for the American people. Serves as a principal technical expert formulating and directing high level, enterprise-wide engineering strategies for Al-enabled systems, computer hardware/software integration, and cloud-native architectures and platforms, delivering impactful solutions for the American people. Provides technical guidance for the development and management of Al-enabled systems, computer hardware/software integration, and cloud-native architectures, ensuring compliance with federal mandates, executive orders, and/or other guidelines. Serves as a strategic authority on Al, data strategy and emerging technologies in collaboration with stakeholders and partners. Provides authoritative consultation and technical guidance to agency personnel, shaping national and international Al, computer engineering and digital policies, directives and mandates. Provides expert IT advice to generate and develop new guidance and direction related to efficiency and effectiveness of mission-critical programs and technology solutions and services within customer agencies. Formulates implementation strategies and policies for agency and governmentwide application. The Technology Transformation Services (TTS) is responsible for providing scalable software products and services to federal agencies.

To qualify, you must have one (1) year of specialized experience. Specialized experience is defined as ALL of the following: Contributing to an agile team in developing, testing, debugging, and maintaining digital products and services for public-facing or enterprise environments, including data engineering, cloud-native architectures, computer hardware/software integration, cybersecurity-by-design, performance requirements, interoperability, and/or secure deployment of emerging technologies. This includes applying modern programming languages, frameworks, and technologies (e.g., Python, Go, JavaScript, TypeScript, Node.js, React, Ruby on Rails, Terraform, Next.js, HTML/CSS, Rust). Contributing to the end-to-end design, development, and deployment of digital products, systems and solutions by integrating emergent technologies and industry best practices.
