Summary
Organizational Location: This position is with the Department of Homeland Security, within U.S. Customs and Border Protection, Office of Information and Technology, Chief Technology Office, located in Ashburn, VA. Learn more about this agency

Duties
Help Joining Customs & Border Protection will allow you to use your expertise in artificial intelligence (AI) to lead initiatives, ensure compliance with CBP requirements, and enhance CBP's mission efficiency and effectiveness by collaborating on AI solutions. This position starts at a salary of $143,913.00 (GS-14, Step 1) to $187,093.00 (GS-14, Step 10). As an Information Technology Specialist (Artificial Intelligence), you will become a key team member of Homeland Security professionals to ensure both security and data integrity, and providing training and guidance on AI use and risk management. Typical work assignments include: Leading and actively participating in a diverse portfolio of projects centered on AI and other emerging technologies within CBP. Ensuring CBP's AI development and subsequent implementation adheres to and exceeds CBP's strict requirements. Identifying potential AI initiatives, systematically tracking their progress, thoroughly evaluating their effectiveness and impact, and establishing a robust governance framework guiding their responsible deployment throughout CBP. Advising CBP management on AI integration to enhance CBP program's efficiency and effectiveness.

Problem Solving - Identifies problems; determines accuracy and relevance of information; uses sound judgment to generate and evaluate alternatives, and to make recommendation You qualify for this position if you meet the basic requirement listed above and possess one year of specialized experience as described below. Experience: You qualify for the GS-14 grade level if you possess 1 year of specialized experience equivalent to at least the next lower grade level, performing duties such as: Applying comprehensive AI knowledge (e.g., machine learning, NLP, large language models) to support organizational AI initiatives. Assisting in the development, implementation, and monitoring of AI solutions, ensuring adherence to security, data integrity, and quality standards. Conducting technical and operational analyses of AI solutions, evaluating design, capabilities, and strategic alignment. Collaborating with teams and stakeholders to define AI project requirements and provide guidance on AI use, compliance, and risk. Assisting in the review and development of AI policies to address technological advancements and security vulnerabilities.
